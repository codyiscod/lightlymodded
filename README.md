![Improving, not Overhauling](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/de089ae0144cbea0ac3b66d28061d5cc185d8c02.png)
<br><br>
**The goal of Lightly Modded** is to create a more **polished** and **performant version of Minecraft: Java Edition**. Apart from some unnoticable optimisations, there are **no mods that alter Vanilla gameplay**.

I've played my fair share of _"Quality of Life"_ modpacks which try to make the game better, but it almost **never feels lightweight or polished**. **So that's why I made Lightly Modded** - to give people the **best** experience possible.

Lightly Modded is supported on **many** versions, so you can add your favourite mods without worrying about the rest! **It's quick and easy.**

---

![Multiplayer?](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/81ba1d3d6e3ae4f0625acb14f3a782117b5c16ab.png)
<br><br>
Yes, you can still play on Vanilla servers. But **please** check for any mods which are disallowed on your server. **This modpack can do things vanilla players can't!**

<details>
<summary>Mods which could be detected as "cheating"</summary>

- ⚠️ [Accurate Block Placement](https://modrinth.com/mod/kzwxhsjp) - Place blocks fast and accurately, instead of every 4 ticks
- [Allow Portal GUIs](https://modrinth.com/mod/UVHxud51) - Use your inventory while inside Nether portals
- [Full Bundle Tooltip](https://modrinth.com/mod/VhT3vFnZ) - Show all bundle slots instead of 11-12 slots
- ⚠️ [InvMove](https://modrinth.com/mod/REfW2AEX) - Move while inside of your inventory
- [Inventory Management](https://modrinth.com/mod/F7wXag4i) - Sort inventories and storage
- [LambDynamicLights](https://modrinth.com/mod/yBW8D80W)/Fullbright - See in the dark (generally allowed)
- ⚠️ [Visual Health](https://modrinth.com/mod/X01iuqRJ) - Adds visible damage textures to hurt mobs and players

</details>
